### Invites

We run chaos.social on a vaguely web-of-trust basis:
Everybody with a chaos.social accoung can issue invites for people they think would be a good fit for the instance.

As a result, we do not hand out invite links in our role as instance admins. Please ask your friends for an invite instead!
